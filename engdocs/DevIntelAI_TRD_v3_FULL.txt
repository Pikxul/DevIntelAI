
DEVINTEL AI

=================================================================
1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
=================================================================

DevIntel AI is an AI-Powered Software Delivery Intelligence Platform that integrates
with GitHub repositories, CI/CD pipelines, deployment systems, and operational
tooling to provide deployment intelligence, governance automation, risk analysis,
incident intelligence, and engineering visibility.

The platform is not intended to replace GitHub Actions, Jenkins, GitLab CI, or
other delivery systems. It acts as an intelligence, governance, and analytics layer
on top of existing software delivery infrastructure.

Primary objectives:
- Reduce deployment failures
- Improve release confidence
- Accelerate incident response
- Improve engineering productivity
- Enable enterprise governance
- Centralize operational intelligence
