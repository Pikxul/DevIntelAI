DevIntel AI — User Story & Product Story

Imagine Acme Corp, a growing SaaS company with 80 engineers.

The CTO, John, is frustrated.

Every week:

Developers push code.
Deployments happen.
Production incidents occur.
Nobody knows who approved what.
Engineering managers struggle to understand delivery risk.
Teams work in silos.

John wants visibility and control without micromanaging developers.

That's when Acme Corp adopts DevIntel AI.

Chapter 1 — Getting Started

John visits DevIntel AI.

Instead of being dropped into a dashboard full of charts, he sees:

"Get Started"

He clicks Sign Up.

He can choose:

Google Authentication
Org Email Authentication

After authentication, John creates his organization:

Organization Name:
Acme Corp

Since John created the organization, he automatically becomes:

Organization Owner
Chapter 2 — Building His Team

John doesn't want everyone to have unlimited access.

So DevIntel AI gives him complete control.

From the Organization Settings page, he manually invites employees.

He adds:

Nobody can register themselves into Acme Corp.

Only organization members invited by John can join.

This ensures strict enterprise security.

The Organization is the tenant, and the organization owner/admin controls who joins and what they can do. Users never get unrestricted access.

Think of the hierarchy like this:

   ↓
Organization (Company)
   ↓

   ↓

   ↓
Permissions
